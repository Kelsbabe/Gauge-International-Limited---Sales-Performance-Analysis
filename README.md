## Project Overview

This analysis focused on identifying key sales trends, customer behavior, top-performing regions, and sales rep performance. It supports business decisions that aim to increase revenue, improve customer satisfaction, and optimize sales operations.

---

## Dataset Story

The dataset, structured in tabular format, includes:

- Customer Type (New/Returning)  
- Sales Zone  
- Sales Rep  
- Product Category  
- Quantity Sold  
- Unit Price  
- Payment Type  
- Sales Amount  
- Date of Sale

The dataset was auto-generated for simulation purposes.

---

## Methodology

- **Data Cleaning**: Verified structure, removed inconsistencies, validated one empty cell.
- **Data Splitting**:  
  - **Independent Variables**: Sales Rep, Region, Product ID, Product Category, Customer Type  
  - **Dependent Variables**: Sales Date, Sales Amount, Quantity Sold, Unit Price

- **Preprocessing**: Dataset structured to maintain data integrity.
- **Analysis Tools**: Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, Dashboard)

## Insights & Recommendations

- **Sales Rep**:  
  - David: Top performer ($1,147,737)  
  - Charlie: Underperformed ($860,811) → Needs training or reassignment

- **Regional Sales**:  
  - North: Highest ($1,369,612)  
  - West: Lowest ($1,154,560) → Apply best practices from North

- **Monthly Sales Trend**:  
  - January: Peak ($495,420)  
  - September: Lowest ($367,838)

- **Product**:  
  - Clothing: Most purchased → Stock more

- **Payment**:  
  - Credit Card: Dominant method ($1.75M+)
